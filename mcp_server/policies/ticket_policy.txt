Coderift Support Ticket Policy

1. Every support ticket must have a valid customer name.
2. Every ticket must belong to one category.
3. Categories:
   - Bug Report
   - Feature Request
   - General Question
4. Ticket statuses are:
   - Open
   - Pending
   - Closed
5. Closed tickets cannot be reopened without manager approval.
6. High priority tickets should be handled before Medium and Low priority tickets.
7. Every ticket update must be logged.
